5. MAME - ST-V driver (src/mame/sega/stv.cpp, MAME 0.289)
==============================================================================
URL:      https://github.com/mamedev/mame
Licence:  LGPL-2.1-or-later, used by Brimir under the GPL-3.0
Used in:  all downloads (src/bridge/stv/stv_cartridge.cpp, stv_io.cpp, stv_game_db.cpp,
          stv_bios.cpp, stv_loader.cpp)

What it covers: the protection register handlers of stv_cartridge.cpp
(ReadProtectionRegister, WriteProtectionRegister) are derived from stv_state::
common_prot_r and common_prot_w, and its 315-5838 connection (IsComp5838Read,
WriteComp5838) from decathlt_prot_r, decathlt_prot_srcaddr_w and init_decathlt;
the input port layout of stv_io.cpp follows the driver; the game list
(stv_game_db.cpp) and the BIOS file list (stv_bios.cpp) are transcribed from its ROM
definitions (names, sizes, CRC-32 values, keys, and which sets have a default
EEPROM image); the descrambling of DaeJeon! SanJeon SuJeon's cartridge
(SanJeonByte in stv_loader.cpp) follows init_sanjeon step for step.

The default EEPROM images (eeprom-shienryu.bin and sss.nv) are not part of Brimir: the
core reads them from the user's own ROM set, as MAME does.
